( Who we are )/ 01

We tell Maharashtra's stories — and carry them to the world.

Founded in 2000, Everest Entertainment is one of Maharashtra's leading media and entertainment companies — and the home of Marathi storytelling.

With a rich library of over 260 films, 50 iconic dramas (Nataks), 800+ songs, and one of India's largest collections of Marathi content, we create, produce, distribute, and showcase stories that entertain audiences across theatres, television, digital platforms, and beyond.

And now through JALLOSH — our consumer-facing brand spanning FAST Channels, Amazon Prime Video Channels, and OTT — we will bring together the entire Marathi entertainment ecosystem, creating a seamless destination for audiences to discover, enjoy, and celebrate Marathi stories, music, and culture.

01 Production Original Marathi & Hindi films, dramas and comedies — developed, shot and finished in-house.
( Original cinema, made in Maharashtra )

From script to the big screen.

It all started in 2009 with Mee Shivajiraje Bhosale Boltoy — Mahesh Manjrekar's ambitiously written script, our first venture into production. It went on to become the highest-grossing Marathi film of its time, and a game-changer for everything that followed.

Since then, Everest has produced 23 feature films across a span of innovative, meaningful and clutter-breaking work — from Happy Journey (a fantasy between siblings) and Coffee Ani Barach Kahi (the unusual romcom that became a cult classic), to Tukaram (a state-award-winning biopic of the revered saint), Aajcha Divas Majha (winner of the National Award for Best Feature Film in Marathi), the much-loved Mumbai Pune Mumbai franchise starring Swapnil Joshi & Mukta Barve, and Amhi Doghi — a progressive adaptation of Gauri Deshpande's story.

The slate has earned 3 National Awards, 15 State Awards, and during the pandemic our little gem Picasso premiered directly on Amazon Prime and went on to earn a special mention at the 67th National Film Awards. The mission stays the same: release three to five new films a year, owned end-to-end from script through marketing.

03 Digital Streaming, OTT licensing and a YouTube presence that reaches millions every month.
( Every story, every screen )

From the theatre to the palm of your hand.

The internet has changed the way we watch movies. A whole generation discovers our films online first. Everest was among the first independent studios with a content output deal at Amazon — and we've kept building from there.

The slate streams across Amazon Prime Video, Netflix, JioCinema and Hungama, while our own YouTube home — Everest Marathi — has crossed 2 million subscribers, making it one of the leading Marathi channels for music, clips and short-form content. Films play on smart TVs, laptops, tablets and mobiles, with subtitles for global audiences.

04 Music Soundtracks and devotional catalogue, distributed across every digital music platform.
( The sound behind the story )

Songs that outlive the credits.

Music has always been an integral part of Marathi storytelling, and Everest Entertainment is proud to curate one of Maharashtra's most celebrated music catalogues. With a repertoire of over 800 songs spanning blockbuster film soundtracks, devotional music, independent releases, romantic melodies, and timeless classics, our collection continues to entertain and inspire audiences across generations.

Our catalogue features memorable music from celebrated films such as Photocopy, Mumbai Pune Mumbai, Tukaram, Maharashtra Shaheer, Boyz, and Pinjra, alongside an extensive collection of devotional and independent music. Featuring the work of acclaimed composers, lyricists, and artists including Ajay–Atul, Avadhoot Gupte, Shreya Ghoshal, Shankar Mahadevan, Shaan, Vaishali Samant, Bela Shende, and many others, our repertoire reflects the richness and diversity of Marathi music.

Whether it's a timeless film melody, a devotional favourite, or a contemporary classic, every song in the Everest collection reflects our enduring commitment to preserving, celebrating, and sharing the very best of Marathi music. Together, they form a living musical legacy — connecting generations through melodies that continue to inspire, entertain, and endure.
